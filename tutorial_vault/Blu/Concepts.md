You know hashtags?

Well concepts are like that except, like everything here, a bit more needlessly complicated.

Concepts start with an underscore:

**_thisIsAConcept**

Clicking on it won't work yet, but after log in all the thoughts containing that concept tag are just a click away.

Concepts can also be nested:

**_gaming_tetris_tactics**

and they take on the color of the thoughts containing it.

Look in the mode menu once you log in;-)
