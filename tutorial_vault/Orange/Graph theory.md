The **graph view** over there is not called that just because it **sounds good**. 

The rendering technique is an old and diverse way to represent __graphs__.

In graph theory it's defined every which way but, simply put:

**A graph is**
  **a set of entities called nodes**
  **interconnected by some kind of relationship.**

Here the entities are thoughts and they are connected by linking each other.

But **graphs can represent** almost **anything**.

A plan of a metro? That's a graph view.
Google maps uses graphs to represent roads and cities.
Printed circuit board? That's a planar graph of conductor lines.
What about you...

**Are you a graph?**

Of course you are! **A graph of** blodvessels and organs and friends and groups and lineages and **ideas**
